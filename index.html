About me

Yijian Qin has been with Huawei's Top Mind Program in Beijing since July 2024, where he works on multimodal foundation models with electromagnetic waves, applied to smart cities, navigation, embodied intelligence, the low-altitude economy and other scenarios.

He received his Ph.D. from the Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University in 2024, advised by Prof. Wenwu Zhu (Fellow of the Academia Europaea), and his B.E. from the same department in 2019.

His research interests include multimodal large models (electromagnetic waves as a modality), recursive self-improvement (extending the autonomy of automated machine learning from architectures and hyper-parameters to the tasks, the data and the evaluation criteria), and AI4Science (molecular, protein and microbiome property prediction). He has published around 20 papers at venues such as NeurIPS, ICML, KDD, AAAI and ICDM, including 5 first-author papers. According to Google Scholar, his work has received about 775 citations with an h-index of 16 (as of Sept. 2026). He is the principal maintainer of AutoGL and the first author of NAS-Bench-Graph, and serves as a reviewer for ICML, NeurIPS and ICLR.
